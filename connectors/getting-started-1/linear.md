# Linear

Sortment connects to Linear, letting Sortment AI access issues, cycles, and projects. Like the Jira connector, this is useful for tying engineering work and release timing to campaign or journey planning.

***

### How to use

1. Click the workspace name in the top right.
2. Go to **Workspace Settings**.
3. Go to **Connectors**.
4. Find **Linear** and select **Connect**. You'll be redirected through Linear's OAuth flow to log in and authorize access.

***

### What can this power

* Checking a feature's status in Linear before timing a related campaign or journey
* Surfacing whether a spike in support tickets or churn coincides with a recent release or open issue
