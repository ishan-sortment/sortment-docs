# Pipedrive

Sortment connects to Pipedrive, letting Sortment AI manage deals, contacts, and pipeline stages. This ties sales pipeline context into the same customer journey view as product and lifecycle data.

***

### How to use

1. Click the workspace name in the top right.
2. Go to **Workspace Settings**.
3. Go to **Connectors**.
4. Find **Pipedrive** and select **Connect**. You'll be redirected through Pipedrive's OAuth flow to log in and authorize access.
