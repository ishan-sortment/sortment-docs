# Airtable

Sortment connects to Airtable, letting Sortment AI read and write records in your Airtable bases. Teams that use Airtable to plan campaigns, track content calendars, or maintain creative asset libraries can pull that structured data into Sortment, and write status updates back without switching tools.

***

### How to use

1. Click the workspace name in the top right.
2. Go to **Workspace Settings**.
3. Go to **Connectors**.
4. Find **Airtable** and select **Connect**. You'll be redirected through Airtable's OAuth flow to log in and authorize access to the bases you want Sortment to use.

***

### What can this power

* Intelligence Blocks that reference Airtable-tracked campaign plans, content calendars, or asset libraries alongside warehouse and engagement data
* Writing execution status or campaign metadata back to Airtable, so it stays the source of truth for planning while Sortment handles targeting and send
