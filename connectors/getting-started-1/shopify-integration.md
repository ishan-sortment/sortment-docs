# Shopify Integration

Sortment connects to Shopify, letting Sortment AI manage orders, products, and customers in Shopify. For any team selling through Shopify, this brings core commerce data directly into the customer journey view alongside marketing and lifecycle engagement.

***

### How to use

1. Click the workspace name in the top right.
2. Go to **Workspace Settings**.
3. Go to **Connectors**.
4. Find **Shopify** and select **Connect**. You'll be redirected through Shopify's OAuth flow to log in and authorize access to your store.

***

### What can this power

* A complete view of the customer journey, from first touch through purchase and repeat order behavior
* Segmenting or triggering lifecycle journeys based on order and product data, like cart abandonment or repeat purchase patterns
