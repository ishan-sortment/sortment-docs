# Stripe

Sortment connects to Stripe, letting Sortment AI access payments, customers, and subscriptions. This brings revenue and billing data directly into the customer journey view, alongside marketing and product engagement.

***

### How to use

1. Click the workspace name in the top right.
2. Go to **Workspace Settings**.
3. Go to **Connectors**.
4. Find **Stripe** and select **Connect**. You'll be redirected through Stripe's OAuth flow to log in and authorize access.

***

### What can this power

* A customer journey view that includes payment and subscription status, not just product and lifecycle events
* Segmenting or triggering journeys based on billing data, like a failed payment or an upcoming renewal
