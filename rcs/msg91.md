# MSG91

Before you begin, you will need an MSG91 RCS account already set up.

#### 1. Find the provider

Go to Settings → Engagement Setup → Sender Profiles. Select the RCS channel, then click Add integration and choose MSG91 as the provider.

#### 2. Configure the integration

In the pop-up, fill in:

* **Custom name**: A name to help you identify the configured account in Sortment's portal.
* **API Key**: Log into your MSG91 RCS account, go to Authkey settings, and copy the Authkey.
* **Project ID**: Found in your MSG91 account settings.

#### 3. Complete the integration

Click Save once done, and you are all set.

### Message delivery status

Sortment can track notification delivery status, but MSG91 requires a manual update of the Callback Endpoint to receive these reports. To update it:

1. Copy the Callback URL from the integration pop-up in Sortment.
2. Send the Callback URL to your MSG91 account manager to add it to your MSG91 account.
