# Contact Us

Need help with Sortment? Our team is here to support you at every step — from onboarding and warehouse setup to building audiences, campaigns, and journeys.

## Reach our support team

For any questions, issues, or feedback, email us at [support@sortment.com](mailto:support@sortment.com). Our team will get back to you as quickly as possible.

{% hint style="success" %}
For the fastest resolution, include your workspace name and a short description of what you were trying to do — screenshots or error messages help too.
{% endhint %}
